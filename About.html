About Us

Here at Peachy Keen Records, we are always striving to provide you with a vast, updated inventory. Established in 2020, we have been working to curate a record store that has a little something for everyone.

Peachy Keen is proud to partner with local artists who help to support the creative arts scene within our community. Without art, the world would be a much duller place, and we want to keep the creativity flowing wherever possible. When you shop at Peachy Keen, you are directly supporting local artists and creators!

When we first opened, we wanted to work as something more than just another record store. That's why we've continued to add extra things to find in our shop, from books, to gifts, to our very own coffee shop. We promise that when you stop into our store, you'll have a Peachy Keen time!
